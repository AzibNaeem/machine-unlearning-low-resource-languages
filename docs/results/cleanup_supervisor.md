## 3. Reconciling ~5% / ~29% (Sup2) with 36.6% / 57.3% (now)
Not a bug; a different probe. Sup2: unregularised linear head (Adam, 300 steps, raw features) trained on retain_sub (the data the model was fit on) plus 300 forget images, imbalanced 300 vs 1000 per class; Sup2 tested classes 2, 5 only, Retrain 20 epochs. Now: standardised L2 logistic regression on 200 held-out images per class, balanced. Measured on the same class-0 models: Retrain floor 23.1 (Sup2 probe), 22.7 (sklearn on the memorised retain_sub data), 50.2 (held-out probe data); DNF-SGD 45.5 / 52.5 / 63.3. So the main driver is training the probe on data the model was fit on (memorised, class-separated features) versus held-out data: about +27 points on the Retrain floor; regularisation and imbalance are secondary. Protocol-independent statement: absolute probe levels (5%, 23%, 37%, 50% for the same Retrain model) mean nothing on their own. What is stable: DNF-SGD keeps about half of the base-over-floor separability under every probe (Sup2 44%, new 55%; class-0 spot check 39-52%) and sits +13 to +30 points above a Retrain model on the same class (+20.7, SE 2.7, 5/5 classes with the held-out probe). Adam methods are at or within a few points of the floor, but so is the no-noise Adam control.

## 5. What step 3 shows
On one overfit CIFAR-10 ResNet-18 base (single seed, class as unit), Acc_f is about 0 for every method, but that mostly reflects the regime. Under SGD, noise samples cause the forgetting (no-noise control Acc_f 78.9; DNF 0.25, USI(F)R 3.7, UNSIR 15.4; 10/10 classes) without retain loss (76.5-77.5 vs 76.8), yet the forgetting is shallow (probe +21 to +27 over Retrain on 5/5 classes, R_t 4-21 epochs, W_D about 5). Every Adam row (and FineTune) is fine-tune drift: the no-noise Adam control has Acc_f 0.00, Acc_r 69.55, W_D 89, and no noise row beats it; noise lowers retain further. Learned noise (UNSIR, USI(F)R) is no better than Gaussian noise at equal count, the paper's ordering and targets (USI(F)R > DNF > UNSIR; 74.97 / 74.45 / 71.06) are not reproduced, and DNF-SGD's config was tuned post hoc on an older base. Unknown: whether noise content or its forget label drives SGD forgetting (no random-label control), whether results survive another base seed or a well-generalising base, how deep forgetting is under a stronger relearn budget, the cause of the missed 74.97 without the authors' code, and whether any of this transfers to embedding-layer noise in XLM-R.
